# rivalr

A persistent study guild platform for Malaysian SPM science stream students. Create private groups with friends, complete quizzes on your own time, and compete on multi-level leaderboards.

## What is rivalr?

rivalr is a quiz platform built around **guilds** — private groups where you and your study partners track progress, compete, and push each other to do better. It's async, so no one has to be online at the same time.

### Guilds

Create a private guild and invite friends via link. Your guild is your home base — see everyone's stats, rankings, and recent activity at a glance.

### Quizzes

Pick a subject, chapter, and difficulty. Questions are AI-generated and aligned to the official SPM syllabus. Answer at your own pace, earn XP, and track your improvement over time.

### Leaderboards

Three levels of competition:
- **Subject** — who's best at Biology, Chemistry, Physics, or Add Math?
- **Chapter** — drill into a specific chapter (e.g. Electrochemistry)
- **Difficulty** — compete head-to-head on Easy, Medium, Hard, or KBAT questions

### Achievements & Progression

Earn XP from every quiz. Level up, unlock achievements, and collect coins to spend in the shop on cosmetic items — avatar frames, sound effects, quiz themes, and taunt stickers.

## Subjects Covered

| Subject | Forms | Chapters |
|---------|-------|----------|
| Biology | 4 & 5 | 9 + 7 |
| Chemistry | 4 & 5 | 9 + 5 |
| Physics | 4 & 5 | 5 + 5 |
| Additional Mathematics | 4 & 5 | 10 + 8 |

All questions follow the official SPM syllabus with four difficulty tiers: Easy, Medium, Hard, and KBAT (Higher Order Thinking).

## Tech Stack

- **Frontend** — React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
- **Auth** — Google OAuth
- **Hosting** — Cloudflare Pages + Workers

## Getting Started

```bash
# Install dependencies
pnpm install

# Start development server
pnpm dev
```
